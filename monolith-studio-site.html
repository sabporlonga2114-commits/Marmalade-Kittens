The studio

We don't do walk-in flash. Every piece is designed once, for one person, and never repeated.

Monolith runs by consultation only. You bring the idea — a memory, a reference, an obsession — and we spend real time turning it into something that will still make sense on your skin in twenty years. No trend pieces, no matching sets, no pieces we've already done for somebody else.
